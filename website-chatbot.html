// The Problem

Your website has questions. It never has answers.

A visitor with a question and no quick answer is a visitor who leaves. Most of them never call.

  • People land on your site after hours, when no one is there to reply.
  • They want one thing answered: do you take my insurance, are you open Saturday, how much is a visit.
  • If they cannot find it in a few seconds, they bounce to a competitor who makes it easy.
  • Even interested visitors leave without giving you a way to reach them.

Where do the answers come from?

From the information you give us: your hours, services, policies, and FAQs. It answers from your real business details rather than guessing.

How do I hear about a new lead?

The moment a visitor shares their name and phone, you get an instant alert so you can follow up while they are still interested.
