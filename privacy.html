SMS / text messaging

AI Bot Coder may send text-message (SMS) notifications to individuals who have provided their mobile number and consented to receive them — for example, internal operational alerts to our own staff, and service-related notifications to customers who opt in.

  • Message frequency: message frequency varies based on account and service activity.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information collected for text messaging is used solely to deliver the messages you have consented to receive. No mobile opt-in data is shared with any third party except service providers strictly necessary to transmit the messages (for example, our SMS carrier), and only for that purpose.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as email, hosting, payment, and messaging providers), and only as needed to deliver our services, or where required by law.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information and retain it only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP, unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message, or request access to or deletion of your information by contacting us.
