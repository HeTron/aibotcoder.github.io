Problem

Job costs were logged by hand, and sensitive data couldn't go to cloud AI tools.

Built

A tool that logs labor, materials, and POs per job into structured Excel, plus a self-hosted local AI server (Jarvis) for internal queries and workflows.

Result

Real-time job costing and a private AI assistant running at zero cloud cost.
