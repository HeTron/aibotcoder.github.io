Patient SMS Consent

How consent is collected for post-visit text messages sent by AI Bot Coder LLC on behalf of its client practices.

AI Bot Coder LLC provides communication software to local businesses such as dental practices. On behalf of those client practices, we send a short follow-up text message after a completed visit, for example a thank-you with an invitation to leave feedback.

How patients opt in

Consent is collected in person, directly by the practice, before any message is sent. When a patient provides their mobile number at scheduling or on the practice's patient intake form, the form includes the following optional consent language:

Intake form consent language Optional: I agree to receive appointment-related and post-visit follow-up text messages (such as a thank-you and feedback request) from [Practice Name] at the mobile number I provided. I understand this is optional, that my consent is not a condition of receiving treatment or any service, that message frequency is at most one follow-up message per visit plus any appointment reminders I request, that message and data rates may apply, and that I can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

Patients who leave the box unchecked still receive full service from the practice and are never sent follow-up text messages.

Program details

  • Program: post-visit follow-up messages (thank-you and feedback requests) sent on behalf of the patient's own practice.
  • Message frequency: at most one follow-up message per completed visit.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help, or contact jason@aibchq.com.
  • Opting out stops all follow-up texts immediately and never affects the patient's care or standing with their practice.
  • See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. Mobile numbers are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

This page documents the consent process used by AI Bot Coder LLC and its client practices. It is informational; opt-in happens in person at the practice, and opt-out is available at any time by replying STOP to any message.
