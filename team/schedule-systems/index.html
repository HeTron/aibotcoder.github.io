What software runs their schedule?

A dental office's practice management software (their "PMS") decides whether our receptionist can book appointments straight into their calendar, needs one extra step, or needs a connector. Ask one question early, write down exactly what they say, and read the room off this guide.

Read this before anything else

The answer never kills the call. You are booking a demo, not building an integration. Every office is worth a demo no matter what software they run.

The software name is intel, not a qualifier. Write it in the Notes field, word for word, exactly how they said it. Jason decides what it means. You keep going.

Ask first

"What system do you use to run your schedule?"

If they sound unsure, add: "the software the front desk opens to see the day." If they still don't know, that is a fine answer. Write "didn't know" and book the demo anyway.

Open door, we connect directly

4 systems

Say"Good, that's one we work with directly, so the receptionist books right into your schedule. Let me show you what that looks like."

Open Dental Live today

Our already-built, running-in-production integration. Books, reschedules, and confirms. Any Open Dental office is a top target, say so with full confidence. You may hear it as "OpenDental" or "OD."

tab32 Open door, not built yet

Cloud software built around open connections. The easiest new one for us to add. Prime prospect. Sounds like "tab thirty-two."

Curve Dental Open door, not built yet

Modern cloud platform, light gating. Straightforward once we're set up with them. They may just say "Curve" or "Curve Hero."

CareStack Open door, not built yet

Cloud, and they publish a public developer portal with appointment access. Common in bigger groups and multi-location practices. Sometimes said as "Care Stack."

CarefulOnly Open Dental is built and live right now. For the other three, say "we work with that" and book the demo. Do not promise a go-live date or say it's already connected.

Workable, one extra step on our side

8 systems

Say"Yes, we support that. There may be one setup step on the integration side, nothing you'd have to manage. Let's get you the demo and I'll flag your system for the team."

Dentrix Ascend Cloud

The cloud Dentrix, a different product from the old desktop one. Reachable through the vendor's approved-partner program. Always confirm which one: "Is that Dentrix Ascend, the browser one, or the version installed on your computers?" That one question changes the whole answer.

Fuse Cloud · Patterson

Patterson's cloud platform, more open than their older software but still partner-gated. Workable.

Denticon Cloud · groups / DSO

Common in multi-location groups. Cloud-friendly, but access runs through enterprise agreements, so it lands best when the group itself is the buyer. Made by Planet DDS.

Dentrix Enterprise Groups · clinics

The large-group and community-clinic version of Dentrix. Built for connecting to other medical systems, but it's an enterprise conversation, not a quick hookup. Flag it as a group deal.

Archy Cloud · newer

Newer cloud-only platform, growing fast with younger practices. Modern architecture is in our favor. We have not confirmed their outside-access policy yet, so note it and let Jason check.

Oryx Cloud · newer

Cloud-only, aimed at independent practices. Same story as Archy: modern and promising, access policy unconfirmed. Note it.

Practice-Web Installed / hosted

Reachable at the database level, but thin documentation and it breaks between versions. Doable for a motivated office, not a system we chase.

Adit Add-on, sometimes the main system

Usually an add-on layer, not the schedule itself. Ask what's underneath it. If Adit truly is their whole system, workable. If it sits on top of Dentrix or Eaglesoft, the older system underneath sets the limit.

Needs a bridge, the wall

5 systems

Say"That one's a little more locked down for outside booking. Two paths there: we can handle every call, greet, answer questions, take messages and route, and we can add live booking through a connector. Let me get you the demo and we'll map the best fit."

Dentrix Installed · the common one

The single most common answer you'll hear. The classic desktop Dentrix, installed on a computer in the office. Outside booking is limited to the vendor's approved partners. Not a lost deal, just don't promise direct booking. Confirm it isn't Ascend before you file it here.

Eaglesoft Installed · Patterson

The second most common. Closed and partner-only, very hard to write bookings into without a bridge. Bridge or answer-only.

Easy Dental Installed · legacy

The older, cheaper sibling of Dentrix, same family. Small and solo practices still run it. Treat like Dentrix.

SoftDent / PracticeWorks Installed · Carestream

Older installed software being phased out by its maker. No open door. Answer-only unless the deal is big enough to justify a connector.

DentiMax Installed · small

Small installed system, mostly known for x-ray imaging. Rare, and closed for outside booking. Log it and move on.

Ortho2 Edge Cloud Workable

Cloud ortho platform. Reported to support outside scheduling connections, which makes it the friendliest of the ortho group. Often said as "Edge" or "Ortho Two."

Cloud 9 Workable

Browser-based, built for ortho, kids' dentistry, and groups. Cloud is in our favor, connections are limited. Note it.

Dolphin Needs a bridge

CS OrthoTrac Needs a bridge

Older installed ortho software, being phased out. No real open door. Not worth chasing for direct booking.

Not a schedule system, ask again

the most common mix-up

Say"Got it, that's the messaging and reviews side. What about the actual schedule, the software the front desk opens to see the day?"

Weave Phone + texting

You will hear this one a lot. It's a phone and texting system that plugs into their real schedule. It is not the schedule. Ask again. Bonus: if they name a phone system, that's useful intel too, note it.

RevenueWell · Solutionreach · Lighthouse 360 Reminders

Patient reminder and messaging tools that sit on top of the schedule. Not the schedule. Ask again.

Yapi · Modento · Dental Intelligence Add-ons

Paperwork, check-in, and reporting add-ons. They read from the real system. Ask what the real system is.

Podium · Birdeye · Swell Reviews

Review and reputation tools. Nothing to do with the schedule. Ask again.

NexHealth · Kolla · Sikka Connectors

These are connectors, the bridge itself. If an office already pays for one, that is genuinely good news for us. Write it down and flag it to Jason, it can make a walled system reachable.

On the call

Ask early

Slip it in while they're still talking, not at the end. It sounds like curiosity, not a form.

On the call

Never react badly

No "oh, that's a problem." Every answer gets the same warm response. You are not qualifying them out.

After the call

Log it word for word

Put the name in Notes exactly as they said it, even if you're unsure of the spelling. Never guess or clean it up.

After the call

Book the demo anyway

The software never decides whether they get a demo. Set the status and the follow-up date like always.

A locked-down office is still a good call

If their software is on the "needs a bridge" list, it does not mean we can't help them. We can still answer every call, answer questions, take messages, and transfer to the front desk, and there are ways to add live booking on top. That is Jason's call to make, not a conversation for the phone. You just write the system name in Notes and book the demo.
