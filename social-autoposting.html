// Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

Do I have to approve every post?

You approve the month as a batch. You see the whole calendar first and can approve all of it or send back edits. Nothing publishes until you sign off.

Will it sound like a generic bot?

No. We set it up with your voice, your services, and the tone you want. The sample calendar on this page shows the style. If a post is not right, you change it before it goes live.

What if I want to change a caption?

Tell us and we update it. You are reviewing the month before anything is scheduled, so edits happen before the public ever sees a post.

Will it flood my page all at once?

No. Posts are spaced out evenly, and each new month is scheduled after your last queued post, so your cadence stays steady instead of doubling up.

Which platforms does it post to?

Facebook and Instagram. You connect your accounts once, approve your calendar, and approved posts publish on schedule.
