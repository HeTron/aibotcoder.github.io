The AI pivot

I trained at 4 Geeks Academy, where I built predictive models, NLP classifiers, and data pipelines in Python. After completing the program, I stayed on as a mentor, helping other students bridge the gap between technical skills and real-world application.

Then Claude Code changed everything. Anthropic's AI coding tool, paired with my background building and running operations, meant I could go from idea to working automation in hours instead of weeks. I've now built production systems for a South Florida general contractor (blueprint estimating, job cost tracking) and deployed a fully local AI server that runs with zero cloud cost.

I built AI BOT CODER to bring that capability to small businesses that need it most, the ones too busy running the business to figure out the tools themselves.
