// Compliance Is The Feature

The vendors who skip this expose you.

Outbound calling has real rules. A cheap tool that ignores them does not save you money, it hands you the liability. Every safeguard here is built in and cannot be turned off.

Protection you can point to.

If it is not safe to call, we do not call. That is the whole design. You get the reactivated leads without the exposure that comes from doing this the reckless way.

  • Consent-checked lists. No record of prior consent means no dial. We verify consent language from your own intake form before any campaign runs.
  • Instant do-not-call handling. The moment someone asks to stop, they are added to a permanent suppression list and never contacted again.
  • Legal calling hours only. Calls go out inside the allowed daytime window in the person's local time, never early morning or late night.
  • One call per lead. Each person is called at most once per campaign. No repeat dialing, no harassment.
  • Clear AI disclosure. The call is upfront and honest about who is calling and why.

Why this is the whole point: the tools that promise the most dials for the least money are usually the ones cutting these corners. When a complaint or a lawsuit lands, it lands on your business, not the vendor. We built this so that never happens.

// Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

How do you know a lead consented to a call?

Before a campaign runs, we verify the consent language on your own lead-capture form. If a lead has no record of prior consent to be contacted, they are never dialed. No consent, no call.

What happens if someone asks to stop?

They are removed immediately and added to a permanent do-not-call list that is checked before every future dial. They will not be contacted again.

When do the calls go out?

Only during the legal daytime calling window in the lead's local time. No early-morning or late-night calls, ever.

Will people get called over and over?

No. Each lead is called at most once per campaign. Re-running a campaign will not double-dial anyone.

What do I get back?

Every lead is tagged with an outcome (interested, not interested, voicemail, or bad number) and you get a summary, so you know exactly who to follow up with.
