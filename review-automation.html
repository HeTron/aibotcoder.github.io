// Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

How does it know who to text?

You tell it a visit happened, either by logging it or sending us a simple list of who visited and their phone number. That visit is what triggers the request.

What stops it from bothering people?

Three guardrails: anyone can reply STOP to opt out for good, a cooldown prevents asking the same person again too soon, and messages only send during quiet daytime hours. Each visit gets at most one request.

Where does the review actually go?

The text links directly to your Google review page. One tap takes the customer straight there.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. The message is written in your voice and names your business. We adjust it whenever you want.

Do I need special software to start?

No. You can begin by sending us a simple list of visits. Deeper connections to your scheduling system can come later.
