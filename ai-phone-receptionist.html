// Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

Will it sound like a robot?

Call the demo at (954) 568-8788 and judge for yourself. The voice is natural and conversational. We do disclose it's an AI at the start of the call, that's a legal requirement, not a downside. Patients adjust within seconds.

What happens if a patient asks something complicated?

The AI transfers to your front desk on demand, patients can say "agent", "person", or just ask. We also configure specific keywords (e.g. "emergency", "billing") to trigger an immediate transfer.

Is patient data stored?

No PHI (patient names, DOB, health info) is stored. Call outcomes are logged at the level of "booked / transferred / info / hung up", nothing identifying. Audio is processed under a signed BAA with our voice provider.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your existing number. We give you a dedicated AI line and you forward calls to it, typically only after hours, or all the time, your choice. You can flip it on and off at any time.

What if I want to change something later?

Email or call us, most updates (hours, FAQs, scripts) ship the same day. There are no per-change fees and no waiting in a support queue.

Can it also call patients to confirm appointments?

Yes, outbound appointment-confirmation calls are part of the system. Same AI voice, fully conversational. We integrate with your scheduling system during onboarding so the AI knows who to call and when. We are fully integrated with Open Dental today, and we confirm what is possible with your system before you commit. Patients can confirm, reschedule, or cancel right on the call, and your calendar updates automatically.
