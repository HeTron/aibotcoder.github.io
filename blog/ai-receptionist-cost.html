How Much Does an AI Receptionist Cost?

Short answer

An AI phone receptionist costs roughly $300–$900 per month for most small and medium businesses. AI BOT CODER's AI Phone Receptionist is $499/mo for inbound and $599/mo for inbound + outbound confirmations, with $0 setup. For comparison, a human answering service runs $1,200–$2,000/mo for less coverage.

What you get at that price

Monthly pricing for a quality AI receptionist is usually all-inclusive. With AI BOT CODER, the $499/mo inbound plan covers everything needed to answer and route calls:

  • A dedicated phone number and 24/7 AI answering — nights, weekends, lunch, holidays
  • Real-time appointment booking onto your calendar
  • A custom greeting and an FAQ knowledge base (hours, location, insurance, services)
  • Transfer-to-human on demand
  • Call-outcome logging (booked / transferred / info / hung up)
  • Setup, onboarding, and same-day config changes — included, no per-change fees

The $599/mo plan adds outbound appointment-confirmation calls — the AI calls patients to confirm, reschedule, or cancel, and updates your calendar automatically.

What makes the price vary

If you're comparing quotes, four things move the number:

  • Call volume — very high-volume lines may cost more; most SMBs sit in the standard range.
  • Outbound calling — adding confirmation/reminder calls is usually a modest step up (here, $100/mo).
  • Integrations — connecting to scheduling or practice-management software (e.g. Open Dental, Dentrix). Note: Open Dental customers pay an additional ~$30/mo directly to Open Dental for API access.
  • Compliance — HIPAA/BAA for medical and dental practices. AI BOT CODER includes HIPAA compliance, a signed BAA, and built-in AI disclosure at no extra charge.

Is it worth it?

The honest test is whether the calls you currently miss are worth more than the monthly fee — and for most call-driven businesses they are, by a wide margin. A single recovered customer often covers the plan for the month.
