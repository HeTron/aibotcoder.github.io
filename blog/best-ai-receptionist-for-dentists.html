What to look for in a dental AI receptionist

Dental practices have specific needs a generic call bot won't meet. Use this checklist when comparing vendors:

  • HIPAA compliance + signed BAA — non-negotiable for any system that touches patient calls.
  • Real-time appointment booking — checks availability and books during the call, not just message-taking.
  • Insurance & FAQ handling — answers "do you take my insurance?", hours, location, and services from a knowledge base you control.
  • Transfer to your front desk on demand — plus emergency keyword routing.
  • After-hours coverage — nights, weekends, lunch, and holidays, when many patients actually call.
  • PMS integration — works with Open Dental, Dentrix, Eaglesoft for outbound confirmations.
  • No patient data stored — outcomes logged without PHI.

Why it matters for dental specifically

In dentistry, a missed call is usually a missed appointment — and an appointment is worth hundreds of dollars. Most callers won't leave a voicemail; they call the next practice. Front desks are also busy with patients in the chair, so calls during lunch and after hours routinely go unanswered. An AI receptionist closes that gap without adding front-desk headcount.
