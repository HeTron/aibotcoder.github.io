// Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

What tools do you use to build automations?

I build with Python, Claude AI (Anthropic), n8n for workflow automation, FastAPI for custom APIs, and integrate with tools you're already using: Google Workspace, Notion, email, Dropbox, and more. No vendor lock-in.

Do I need to be technical to use what you build?

No. Everything I build is designed to be used by non-technical people. You'll get a walkthrough video, clear instructions, and I'm available by email if anything comes up.

How long does it take to build an automation?

Most single automations are delivered within 5-10 business days. Multi-automation packages typically take 2-4 weeks. I'll give you a specific timeline before we start.

What if I'm not sure what can be automated?

That's exactly what the free discovery call is for. Tell me what you do every day that feels repetitive or tedious, I'll tell you what's automatable and what the ROI looks like.

Is there ongoing cost after the project?

The project fee is one-time. Depending on the tools used, there may be small API costs (typically $10-$50/month for most small businesses). The optional $299/month retainer covers maintenance, updates, and ongoing improvements.
